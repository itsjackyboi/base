Check pins for the zoom shift

Before the fix, a zoomed-out map was drawn too far north by 32, 96, 224, 480 or 992 blocks, depending on the zoom. A pin placed there sits that far north of its real spot. Each row shows the pin as placed (gold border), then the spots that far south. Click the tile that shows the right place to move the pin there, or Looks right to keep it. Undo works here too. In water? means the pin's block looks like water, a quick hint rather than proof.
