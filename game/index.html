Pintland Tap

Five places across the Isles, easiest first.

  • A name appears. Tap the map where you think it is. Tap again to move your guess.
  • Pan and zoom as much as you like, then Lock in. A locked guess can't be changed.
  • You get 15 seconds per guess. When time runs out, your guess locks in where it is, or the round scores 0 if you haven't placed one.
  • Closer scores more: 100 for the exact block.
  • One puzzle a day, the same for everyone. It changes at midnight Eastern.
